# M7 — What Would Be Needed to Truthfully Answer “Can I Stay Overnight Here?”

> Hermes report (GPT-5.6 luna), 2026-10-07, stored as returned. HERMES-SOURCED: web research only, no repository access. Nothing here is verified by the coordinator unless another document says so, and nothing is decided.

Access date: 2026-10-07

Scope: White River National Forest, Aspen-Sopris Ranger District; Pike National Forest, South Platte and South Park Ranger Districts; BLM Colorado; Colorado state parks; developed camping, dispersed camping, reservations, restrictions, seasonal status, and vehicle feasibility.

Core result: “Can I stay overnight here?” cannot be answered from a map point, public ownership, a trail, a road, a facility, a campground name, a stocking record, a designation, or a community report. A truthful answer requires a current location-specific permission or reservation rule, all applicable restrictions, current access, vehicle feasibility, seasonal status, and—where applicable—actual inventory.

## What cannot be known from published data today

[UNKNOWN]

1. Whether an arbitrary map coordinate is a legal overnight campsite without intersecting it with the current agency rule, current order, designated-site geometry, and land-management boundary.

2. Whether an apparent Forest Service road has a current dispersed-camping corridor designation. The retrieved national MVUM service exposes vehicle and seasonal route data, but a clearly documented national dispersed-camping attribute was not found.

3. Whether a published campsite or facility is vacant on a requested night. RIDB’s public documentation and data-use pages were not fully loadable in this session, and no verified public `AVAILABILITY` field or live inventory endpoint was established.

4. Whether a route is physically passable today. MVUM data describe legal motorized designation and dates, not snow, mud, washouts, fallen trees, road width, or current gate condition.

5. Whether a state-park reservation can be made for a date without querying the official CPWShop process. CPW’s public camping page establishes reservation requirements and the booking channel, not a verified public availability feed.

6. Whether a current fire restriction applies exactly at a point where federal, county, municipal, private, or special-district boundaries overlap. DFPC directs users to the local sheriff, fire department, or federal land agency.

7. Whether a Forest Service order has been rescinded, superseded, or amended after the page’s displayed update date unless the current alert and associated order are checked at answer time.

8. Whether the user’s vehicle is feasible for a route from route geometry and vehicle class alone. The answer additionally needs vehicle dimensions, trailer length, clearance, road condition, gate status, and any campground-specific length rule.

9. Whether a trail, road, ramp, facility, public ownership layer, designation, stocking record, or community report carries permission for overnight camping or the chosen activity. None of those facts substitutes for a permission statement from the responsible agency or operator.

10. Whether an overnight permit, wilderness quota, bear-container requirement, group-size rule, or parking reservation applies at a particular Aspen-Sopris wilderness or scenic-area location without checking the specific zone and current permit page.

## Recommendation

Implement an overnight answer as a restriction-first evidence chain, not as a single campsite-status field:

1. Identify the managing agency and exact parcel or facility.
2. Check current closure and occupancy/use orders before checking positive camping evidence.
3. Check the official camping rule for the location: developed campground, designated dispersed site, wilderness zone, reservation-only site, or no-camping corridor.
4. Check current fire restrictions and any special resource, wildlife, water-quality, or seasonal closure.
5. Check MVUM route class and seasonal dates, then current road/gate conditions.
6. Check campground length and vehicle rules against the user’s actual vehicle.
7. Query the official reservation channel for the requested dates; label inventory as unknown if no verified availability response is available.
8. Return only one of: “current official rule supports overnight use,” “current official rule prohibits overnight use,” or “unknown—verify with the agency/operator.” Do not convert existence, ownership, route designation, or a map symbol into permission.
