Access date: 2026-10-06

## Operating rules

- RETRIEVED means the source, metadata, terms, or endpoint was read during this session.
- INFERRED means a milestone, difficulty, or product-value judgment based on retrieved evidence.
- UNKNOWN means the session did not establish the fact.
- A mapped feature, ownership polygon, trail, road, facility, boat ramp, fishery designation, or community observation does not by itself establish permission, public access, current legality, or suitability.
- Restrictions and agency-specific rules must be joined before presenting an opportunity as available. COTREX itself warns that depicting a road, trail, or area does not imply that it is passable, maintained, safe, or open for general public use. [RETRIEVED: COTREX Terms](https://trails.colorado.gov/terms)
- PAD-US public-access attributes are generalized and may be incomplete; the USGS says individual agencies remain the official source for their land data and may update more frequently. [RETRIEVED: PAD-US Web Services](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/gap-analysis-project/science/pad-us-web-services)
- Forest Service land and road layers are explicitly not legal documents and must not be used alone to determine ownership, jurisdiction, or restrictions. [RETRIEVED: USFS GIS disclaimer](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/data-tools/gis)

## Sources to avoid

- **COTREX scraping or reverse-engineering without written permission.** Its terms prohibit copying, geographic-data augmentation, crawling, and non-interface access. [Terms](https://trails.colorado.gov/terms)
- **COMaP redistribution without an explicit license.** Its terms prohibit redistribution and warn that data access does not create land-access rights. [Terms](https://comap.cnhp.colostate.edu/terms-of-use/)
- **CAIC automated scraping or data mining.** The retrieved terms expressly prohibit it. [Terms](https://avalanche.state.co.us/terms-use)
- **Commercial map tiles or APIs used as a substitute for licensed source data.** OSM data are open under ODbL, but OSM’s own public tile/API services have separate usage policies. [OSM license](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright/en-EN)
- **Unlicensed third-party community apps, review sites, social posts, or scraped reports.** They do not establish permission, legal access, current restrictions, or rights to reuse content.
- **A source with only a blank license field.** Treat reuse permission as UNKNOWN until the publisher confirms it.
- **Any source that combines roads, trails, parcels, or land-management polygons into an “open” result without agency restrictions.** The underlying sources separately warn that mapped presence does not establish legal access or current conditions. [USFS disclaimer](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/data-tools/gis) · [COTREX Terms](https://trails.colorado.gov/terms)
- **Reservation availability endpoints discovered through browser inspection rather than documented APIs.** Use only documented endpoints or written permission.
- **Community observations presented as authoritative closures, access permissions, or safety judgments.** Use them only as clearly labeled observations with provenance and expiry.

## Recommendation

Proceed with M5 using PAD-US, BLM Surface Management Agency, USFS Basic Ownership, Colorado’s parcel composite, and pilot-county GIS sources, while preserving each source’s authority and disclaimer separately.

Proceed with M6 using USFS MVUM roads/trails, BLM GTLF, RIDB, NWS, USFS alerts, BLM restrictions, DFPC, and NIFC. Build restrictions as first-class records with source, jurisdiction, geometry, effective time, expiry, confidence, and link to the controlling order.

Do not ingest COTREX, COMaP, CAIC, CPWShop, CDOT live feeds, or county reservation feeds until written licensing and machine-access terms are resolved. Treat COTREX as the highest-value partnership target, not as a scraping target.

Keep M7 focused on snow, road-condition, closure reconciliation, and permit/reservation semantics. Defer M8 first-party observations until the product has explicit consent, licensing, privacy, provenance, moderation, takedown, and “observation is not permission” controls.
