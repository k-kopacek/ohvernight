# Track C — Authoritative Recreation Signal Sources

**Milestone:** M4, Functional Recreational Water  
**Geography:** Colorado, emphasizing Aspen/Pitkin County and Douglas County  
**Access date:** 2026-10-06  
**Method:** 12 web searches; primary agency pages, ArcGIS REST metadata, and official machine-readable datasets were preferred.

## Core interpretation rules

- **RETRIEVED:** A source was read during this session.
- **INFERRED:** A conclusion derived from retrieved material, not stated directly.
- **UNKNOWN:** The evidence retrieved did not establish the claim.
- A fishing designation, stocking record, facility point, or nearby recreation site does **not** independently establish legal access, parking, ownership, boating, swimming, or camping permission.

## Concrete findings for named waters

### Aspen and Pitkin County

- **RETRIEVED — Ruedi Reservoir:** USFS expressly lists motorized and non-motorized boating, lake/pond fishing, ice fishing, a boat ramp, and nearby campgrounds. All boats entering and leaving must be inspected; the ramp is closed to boat traffic when inspectors are absent. The page was last updated 2026-03-06: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/whiteriver/recreation/ruedi-reservoir. The CPW station query showed **Ruedi Reservoir Boat Ramp closed for the season** on the access date: https://services5.arcgis.com/ttNGmDvKQA7oeDQ3/arcgis/rest/services/PublicWID/FeatureServer/0/query?where=AuthLoc%20like%20%27%25Chatfield%25%27%20OR%20AuthLoc%20like%20%27%25Ruedi%25%27%20OR%20AuthLoc%20like%20%27%25Aurora%25%27%20OR%20AuthLoc%20like%20%27%25Cherry%20Creek%25%27&outFields=*&returnGeometry=true&f=pjson.

- **RETRIEVED — Fryingpan River and Roaring Fork River:** CPW designates the Fryingpan from “Reudi Res. dam to Roaring Fork River” and the Roaring Fork from “Fryingpan River to Colorado River” as Gold Medal segments: https://services5.arcgis.com/ttNGmDvKQA7oeDQ3/ArcGIS/rest/services/CPWAdminData/FeatureServer/3/query?where=1%3D1&outFields=*&returnGeometry=false&f=pjson. This supports fishery designation only. CPW separately lists Roaring Fork River as a Quality Water with public access, but does not establish access at every bank or parcel: https://cpw.state.co.us/fishing/quality-waters.

- **RETRIEVED — Roaring Fork launch/access facilities:** The Pitkin CSV includes named features such as Jaffee Park Boat Launch, Wingo Junction River Launch, Roaring Fork Meadows River Access, and Roaring Fork Club Angler Parking Lot, with parking/trailer/restroom fields: https://maps.pitkincounty.com/downloads/gis/OST_Points/csv/OST_Points.csv. **INFERRED:** These should be modeled as facility-to-water relationships, not blanket public access along the river.

- **RETRIEVED — North Star:** County commercial access is limited to designated corridors; launching or taking out at North Star Beach is prohibited, and low-water closures can apply: https://pitkincounty.com/DocumentCenter/View/32277/North-Star-RFP-2025. That commercial permit document does not independently define all private recreational use.

- **RETRIEVED — Maroon Lake area:** Reservations are required for personal-vehicle or shuttle access to the Maroon Bells Scenic Area; parking reservations and some overnight permits are required: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/whiteriver/recreation/maroon-bells-scenic-area. **UNKNOWN:** The retrieved page did not authorize fishing, boating, paddling, or swimming in Maroon Lake.

### Douglas County and nearby waters

- **RETRIEVED — Chatfield Reservoir:** CPW authorizes boating, paddleboarding, fishing, two boat-ramp complexes, and seasonal swimming at the designated beach. Registration, ANS stamp, and pre-launch inspection apply to relevant boats. The reservoir is closed to all boats and paddlecraft from December 1 until ice-off through March 1: https://cpw.state.co.us/state-parks/chatfield-state-park/chatfield-state-park-park-highlights.

- **RETRIEVED — Cheesman Reservoir:** Fishing is allowed **only** on the Goose Creek Arm; all boating, camping, swimming, wading, scuba diving, windsurfing, and water-skiing are prohibited. The site is closed October 1–April 30: https://www.denverwater.org/recreation/cheesman-reservoir. This is a harmful-positive case: do not derive boating or general reservoir fishing from water existence or nearby trails.

- **RETRIEVED — Strontia Springs Reservoir / Waterton Canyon:** Canoeing, kayaking, tubing, rafting, boating, swimming, and wading are prohibited. Fishing rules vary by South Platte segment, and reservoir fishing is referred to CPW rules: https://www.denverwater.org/recreation/waterton-canyon-strontia-springs-resevoir. Camping is not permitted in the canyon; limited USFS camping exists above the reservoir on the Colorado Trail: same URL.

- **RETRIEVED — Rueter-Hess Reservoir:** Water recreation requires reservations and is currently offered Friday through Monday. Fishing is restricted to authorized areas, artificial flies/lures, one pole, and no live bait; selected species may be kept while others must be released. Approved small craft may use limited electric trolling motors only for fishing, and all watercraft receive ANS inspection: https://www.douglas.co.us/rueter-hess-recreation/. No dogs are allowed on shore, in the water, or on watercraft: https://www.douglas.co.us/parks-trails-building-grounds/rueter-hess-reservoir/.

- **RETRIEVED — Aurora Reservoir:** Non-motorized craft and electric motors are permitted; gas motors are prohibited. A seasonal ramp exists, trailered craft require inspection, and designated-beach swimming runs seasonally with water-quality closures possible: https://www.auroragov.org/things_to_do/reservoirs/aurora_reservoir/boating-_aurora_reservoir and https://www.auroragov.org/things_to_do/reservoirs/aurora_reservoir/swimming.

- **RETRIEVED — Cherry Creek Reservoir:** CPW supports boating, fishing, two ramps, marina facilities, and a seasonal designated swim beach. The page announces closure to all boating, including paddlecraft, after 2026-11-01; the west ramp was already seasonally closed when retrieved: https://cpw.state.co.us/state-parks/cherry-creek-state-park/cherry-creek-state-park-park-highlights.

## Recommendation

Use a layered evidence model rather than a single `recreation=true` flag:

1. Ingest CPW Fishing Atlas, Gold Medal, stocking, WID, USFS EDW, RIDB, and Pitkin OST records as separate source observations.
2. Store distinct relationships for `fishing_designation`, `stocked`, `regulated_fishing`, `launch_exists`, `inspection_required`, `boating_permitted`, `swimming_area`, `access_point`, `parking`, `camping`, and `closed/restricted`.
3. Prefer stable source IDs (`GlobalID`, `RECAREAID`, `SITE_CN`, `OBJECTID`) and water codes where verified; otherwise require name-plus-spatial matching and manual review.
4. Give operator prohibition and closure pages precedence over generalized recreation points or proximity.
5. Seed explicit harmful-positive rules for Cheesman, Strontia Springs/Waterton, Chatfield’s winter boating closure, Cherry Creek’s seasonal closure, and Ruedi’s inspection-dependent ramp operation.
6. Leave Grizzly, Wildcat, Maroon Lake water use, and Aurora-Rampart as **unknown** until an official manager source expressly establishes permission.
