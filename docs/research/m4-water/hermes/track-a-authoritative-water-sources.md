# M4 Research Report: Authoritative Recreational-Water Sources

Access date: 2026-10-06

## Interpretation boundary

**RETRIEVED.** None of the reviewed hydrography, naming, ownership, facility, or recreation datasets makes every water feature legally accessible. Geometry, a name, public ownership, a mapped ramp, or an activity classification must not independently become an access, fishing, boating, swimming, parking, or camping permission. CPW itself notes that waters may close temporarily and that some property-specific regulations are not mapped.  
Sources: https://cpw.state.co.us/bodies-water-finder and https://ndismaps.nrel.colostate.edu/fishingatlas/definitions.html

## Licensing and reuse findings

- **RETRIEVED.** The National Map data are “free and in the public domain”; USGS requests the acknowledgement: “Map services and data available from U.S. Geological Survey, National Geospatial Program.” https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-are-terms-uselicensing-map-services-and-data-national-map
- **RETRIEVED.** Colorado Information Marketplace applications must carry the prescribed State disclaimer and the State may discontinue feeds. https://data.colorado.gov/terms
- **RETRIEVED.** BLM requests citation and disclosure of modifications. https://gis.blm.gov/arcgis/rest/services/recreation/BLM_Natl_Recreation_Sites_Facilities/MapServer/info/iteminfo
- **UNKNOWN.** Dataset-specific reuse terms were not established for CPW Fishing Atlas, CWCB, Pitkin County, Denver Water, USBR RISE or the USFS recreation layer. Federal public-domain principles may apply, but third-party and contractor inputs prevent assuming that without dataset metadata.

## Ranked candidates

### Canonical water geometry and identity

1. **3DHP flowlines and waterbodies** — current national program, statewide extensibility and explicit refresh information.
2. **GNIS Feature ID and official/variant names** — identity/name authority, joined without treating GNIS points as geometry.
3. **NHDPlus HR/NHD identifiers and network attributes** — migration and temporary gap fallback only.
4. **CDSS `wdid` structures/reservoir records** — authoritative Colorado infrastructure identity, linked through GNIS where available.
5. **Pitkin and Douglas hydrography** — local QA and discrepancy resolution, not statewide truth.

### Recreation enrichment

1. **CPW Fishing Atlas, Quality/Gold Medal waters, regulations, ramps and CPW public-access properties.**
2. **Managing-agency records:** CPW state parks, USFS recreation sites, BLM recreation sites, USBR facility pages and USACE project pages.
3. **RIDB/Recreation.gov**, subject to API-agreement review and provenance retention.
4. **CWCB Environmental and Recreation**, only after open endpoint, metadata and licence are restored.
5. **OSM**, discrepancy/reference use only.

## Unknowns and things I could not verify

- Whether 3DHP has complete EDH-derived coverage in both pilot regions, and which identifier USGS guarantees as persistent across replacement of NHD-derived features.
- CPW layer refresh schedules, stable identifier guarantees, and dataset-specific reuse terms.
- CWCB’s current accessible endpoint, field domains, update date and licence; direct service access required a token or returned not-found.
- USACE NID metadata and terms because its layer endpoint returned 503.
- Open GIS licences for Pitkin County, Denver Water, USBR RISE and the exact USFS layer.
- A machine-readable statewide Denver Water recreation dataset.
- Whether any individual water is presently open. Seasonal closures, private inholdings, permits, licences, entry fees and activity-specific rules require manager-level verification.

## Recommendation

**INFERRED.** Build canonical water entities from 3DHP geometry plus GNIS identity, retaining NHD/NHDPlus crosswalks and CDSS structure IDs. Store every recreation assertion separately with activity, managing authority, source URL, retrieved date, effective/seasonal dates, restrictions and confidence. CPW should lead Colorado fishing and boating enrichment, followed by the relevant land or facility manager.

Never derive permission from geometry, name, ownership, adjacency, a community tag, or another activity. Represent unverified access, fishing, boating, swimming, parking and camping independently as `unknown`, and require an official manager source before changing any one of them to permitted.
