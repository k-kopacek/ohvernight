**Subject:** Reuse of CPW Fishing Atlas / ArcGIS data in a public mapping project — request for terms

Hello,

I maintain Ohvernight, a small open-source web map that helps people find
outdoor recreation and overnight options in Colorado. Its code and data are
public on GitHub. I would like to show a small amount of CPW information
about waters — for example boat ramps, Gold Medal waters and waters with
special fishing regulations — next to water features on the map, with clear
attribution and a link back to CPW for current rules.

Before using any of it I want to be sure I have CPW's permission. The
services I am asking about are:

- the Fishing Atlas map services at `ndismaps.nrel.colostate.edu`
  (`FishingAtlas/FishingAtlas_Data` and `FishingAtlas2025/FishingInfo2025`);
- CPW's ArcGIS Online layers such as `CPWAdminData` (Gold Medal streams) and
  the public Watercraft Inspection Stations layer.

Could you tell me whether the following are permitted, and on what terms?

1. Automated retrieval from these services, at a low rate, a few times a
   year.
2. Storing a transformed subset (selected records and fields for parts of
   Pitkin and Douglas counties to begin with).
3. Publishing those derived, selected records in a public repository and in
   the public map.
4. The attribution and any disclaimer you require, and where it must appear.
5. How often the layers are updated, and whether any identifier (for example
   the water code) is intended to be stable.
6. Any restriction on commercial use. The project is not commercial today; I
   would like to know if that would ever matter.

The Colorado Information Marketplace lists the Fishing Atlas as "Public
Domain", but the map services themselves carry no licence text, so I did not
want to assume.

I will not present CPW data as a statement of what is allowed at a water:
the map always directs people to CPW's current regulations. If there is a
better contact for this question, I would be grateful to be pointed to them.
