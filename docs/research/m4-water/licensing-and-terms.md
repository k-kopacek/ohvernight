# Licensing and terms

Findings in one place. Quoted language was retrieved by Hermes on 2026-10-06
unless marked as checked by the coordinator. A blank or missing licence is
not permission.

| Source | Finding | Where |

## Risks

1. **CPW terms are the main open question.** CPW is the most valuable
   recreation source and its reuse terms are not confirmed. This should be
   answered before any CPW record is committed to the repository; asking CPW
   directly is the reliable route.
2. **Operator rules are prose.** Recording "boating prohibited" with a link
   states a fact and cites it. That is how the repository already records
   reviewed rules. It needs a person's review and a review date, and it goes
   stale.
3. **Community platforms prohibit the obvious approach.** See
   [community-signals.md](community-signals.md).
4. **NHD is frozen.** Public domain, but unmaintained since October 2023.
   Errors in it will not be corrected at source.
5. **Attribution.** Every source used must be added to the attribution in
   `DATA-LICENSE.md` and the source list shown in the app.
