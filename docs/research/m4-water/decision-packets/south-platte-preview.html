South Platte River, Douglas County: projected geometry by option

Offline prototype for the owner decision packet. Every line is source geometry from the USGS National Hydrography Dataset; nothing is invented or joined. Option A (the approved rule on current data) draws nothing and has no panel. North is up; the west edge of Douglas County is shown, Cheesman Lake at the bottom and Chatfield Lake at the top.
