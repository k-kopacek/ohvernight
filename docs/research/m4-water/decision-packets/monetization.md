## DECISION
Should Ohvernight keep all basic permission, restriction, source, freshness
and uncertainty information free, and charge only for evidence-backed
planning workflows, saved-trip assurance and offline convenience?

## OPTIONS
- **A — Free evidence, paid planning.** The map and every fact stay public; comparing and organising complete candidate plans is paid.
- **B — Free evidence, paid trip assurance.** Adds saved-trip monitoring and offline preparation between choosing a plan and leaving.
- **C — Free evidence, seasonal or per-trip toolkit.** The same paid features sold for a trip or a season, for people who do not want another annual subscription.

## RECOMMENDATION (Hermes)
Adopt the free-versus-paid boundary now. Validate Option A first. Treat
Option B as the target paid bundle once saved-plan monitoring can be run
honestly. Do not gate layers. Do not sell the evidence itself to consumers.
First customer: the frequent Colorado activity-first weekend camper.
Competitor price bands found, as reference only: roughly $30–60 a year for
mainstream planning and offline subscriptions, $60–100 for specialist tiers.

## COORDINATOR'S NOTE
- The boundary matches the trust principles: nothing about whether something is allowed, restricted or unknown sits behind payment.
- Monitoring is an operating commitment. A paid alert that misses a closure is worse than no alert. It should not be sold before source monitoring exists and its coverage can be stated honestly.
- Hermes found no evidence that people pay for evidence quality by itself. The paid value is saved work and assurance.
- Nothing in M4 depends on this decision. It shapes M8 and anything after it.

## WHAT CHANGES IF APPROVED
A stated principle: facts free, workflow paid. No code, no pricing, no billing.

## WHAT REMAINS UNCHANGED
The product is not monetised. The roadmap is unchanged.

## TESTS REQUIRED
The validation experiments in the full packet and in
[product-bets.md](../../product-opportunities/product-bets.md), before any
billing is built.

## ROLLBACK
A principle can be revisited at any time before anything is charged for.
