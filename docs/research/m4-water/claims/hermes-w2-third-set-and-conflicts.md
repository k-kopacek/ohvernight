## Recommendation

1. **Publish now:**  
   - Deckers corridor hours, designated parking, day-use reaches, private-bank no-stopping rule, and expressly named fishing/kayaking access.  
   - Cheesman Canyon’s CPW artificial-only and upper-reach catch-and-release rules, with exact reach boundaries.  
   - North Star float-through rules, designated take-outs, dusk-to-dawn closure, west-bank prohibition, and boat-only fishing.  
   - Maroon Bells–Snowmass 100-foot camping setback; Capitol Lake and Thomas Lakes quarter-mile designated-site rule; permit-zone and outside-zone registration requirements.  
   - Ruedi’s 2026 motorized-launch closure and continued hand-launched nonmotorized use.  
   - Fryingpan artificial-only, non-brown-trout release, and brown-trout bag/maximum-size rule directly from CPW.

2. **Do not draft as permitted:** Castlewood water use, Cherry Creek outside the park, Waterton Canyon, Roxborough water use, Douglas County Bear/Plum Creeks, the four Aspen-area creeks, Linkins/Anderson/Petroleum/Willow/Williams/Ivanhoe Lakes, or Twin Lakes.

3. **Use reach- and area-specific records rather than whole-water booleans.** This is necessary for Cheesman Canyon, Waterton, North Star, Chatfield, Fryingpan, and every Maroon Bells–Snowmass permit or setback rule.

4. **Give controlling documents priority but preserve conflicts:** current dated orders and CPW regulations over static recreation summaries; current alerts over ordinary schedules. Do not silently resolve the Ruedi closure-hour, Fryingpan size, North Star/Wildwood, or Maroon Bells dog discrepancies.

5. **Require a fresh official-page check before publication** for Waterton, Wildwood, seasonal North Star closures, Ruedi motorized launching after October 31, 2026, and every status presently marked **UNKNOWN** or **AMBIGUOUS**.
