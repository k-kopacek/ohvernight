# Product opportunity research

Market and customer research by Hermes, requested by the owner on 2026-10-06
as a standing second role for Hermes: look for unmet needs, painful
multi-app workflows and features people would credibly pay for, with evidence
for every idea.

Status: research inputs. Nothing here changes `ROADMAP.md`, any
specification or any milestone. The coordinator and the owner decide roadmap
changes. The trust principles are not negotiable for any idea listed.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [idea-fairy-report.md](idea-fairy-report.md) | The synthesis: ranked pain, multi-app workflows, scored opportunities in tiers, why someone would pay, moats, pricing observations, implications for M4–M8, questions for the owner, suggested experiments |
| [hermes/p1-competitive-landscape-and-pricing.md](hermes/p1-competitive-landscape-and-pricing.md) | Competitor map, pricing table, coverage matrix, white space |
| [hermes/p2-customer-pain-and-workflows.md](hermes/p2-customer-pain-and-workflows.md) | Pain themes, app-switching workflows, unmet feature requests, what people say they pay for |

How to read it:

- Hermes ran with web search and page reading only, under a bounded method.
  Findings are marked RETRIEVED, INFERRED or UNKNOWN by Hermes. The
  coordinator has not re-checked the cited pages.
- User sentiment is paraphrased. Counts of sources are counts of pages read,
  not of people.
- Prices were read on 2026-10-06 and will change.
- Scores are judgements, not measurements. The report itself says it found no
  evidence that people pay for evidence quality on its own.
