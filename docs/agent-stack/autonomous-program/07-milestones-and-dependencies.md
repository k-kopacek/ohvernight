## M5 — Land Classification v1

Deliver separate identities for ownership/management/jurisdiction, public-access evidence and precision/coverage. Federal/state/local/non-federal/private classifications only as source supports. No claim `non-federal = private` or `public = accessible`. Preserve identities across Colorado packages; provide reviewed relationship candidates and per-area evidence-check coverage. Close with independent product scorecard and Douglas scenario.

## M6 — Trails and motorized/recreation uses

Authoritative upstream source and licensing policy must be approved; **COTREX is conditional on terms**, not authorized automatically by roadmap text. Deliver stable user-facing trail/route identity across canonical source segments and region boundaries, duplicate reconciliation with traceable originals, activity-specific use designations and seasonal interpretation, trailhead/staging identity, restrictions/closures and honest access relationships. Distinguish road-legal vehicle status from motorized trail designations; published dates are not current confirmation. Mobile trail detail must show decision-relevant facts early without overstating trust. Close with scenario and independent review.

## M7 — Developed/dispersed camping and day use

Deliver identity and source-backed facts for developed sites, dispersed areas, separate day-use/picnic facilities, stay/season/fee/amenity/restriction claims, available or unknown reservation requirements, and route/vehicle constraints where evidenced. Do not imply camping permission from listing, nearby public land, fee blank or geometry. Preserve alternatives/backup where independent evidence supports them; otherwise explicitly unknown. Include forest orders as evaluated evidence entities only after proper spec, source and review semantics. Close with scenario and independent review.

## M8 — Choose Your Adventure

Deliver an end-to-end trip intent → candidate assembly → evidence-backed ranking/alternatives → unresolved checks flow. Candidate links among activity, trailhead/staging/access, day-use/overnight and location are typed and source-backed/reviewed/unknown. Ranking cannot launder unknown to verified or penalize restrictions invisibly. Explore remains independent continuous discovery. Transparent itinerary, warnings, and ability to inspect original source. Test core scenarios in `01-target-product.md` and the canonical product scorecard. No bookings/transacting implied.

## Colorado coverage program — expansion of delivery, not a new legal-claim milestone

After pilot contract/source/identity validation and owner-approved spec, expand geography incrementally through region packages with a small coverage index and per-view lazy loading. Prefer a third region/edge overlap proof before many counties. A statewide label means map navigability and explicit coverage, NOT all activity/permission claims verified statewide. 3DHP migration is a separate compatibility gate for water (existing M4 debt) before scaling the retired NHD approach blindly.

## Work ordering

Critical-path baseline: finish in-flight M4; M4-C and product baseline; M5 management/land identities; M6 trail identities and motorized/use evidence; M7 developed/dispersed/day-use and relationships; M8 trip candidate assembly; Colorado index and regional scale when cross-boundary contracts are proven. Coordinator may overlap independent **research and contract prototyping** where permitted, but not ship future-milestone production without adopted authorization/spec.

The coordinator may propose reordering or parallelizing implementation once adoption empowers it; it must record why user-facing outcome and safety improve, and retain stage gates. Do not equate a milestone's technical completion with user success.
