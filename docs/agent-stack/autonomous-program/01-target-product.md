# 01 — Target product definition: Colorado-first Ohvernight

## Mission

Answer, on a phone and without false certainty:

> Where can I do the activities I care about, how do I get there, where can I reasonably stay overnight nearby, what restrictions apply, and what is still unknown?

Ohvernight combines recreational discovery and trip assembly, not merely a collection of map overlays. Colorado is the first statewide market. This charter is not authority for national expansion or new commercial services.

## Two complementary experiences

**Explore:** One continuous Colorado map. A user pans/searches across region/county boundaries without selecting a pilot app. Discover trails, water, land context, recreation sites, trailheads/staging, overnight options and restrictions in per-view, lazily loaded packages. Show boundaries of evidence coverage by topic. Uncovered does not mean empty, closed or unrestricted.

**Choose Your Adventure:** The user supplies location/region or destination, approximate dates, activities, day-use versus overnight intent, lodging/camping preference, and relevant vehicle/access constraints. Produce ranked **candidate outings**, with explainable activity + place + access/staging + overnight/day-use links, based only on supported relationships. Each result must show decisive evidence, restrictions, missing checks, unresolved unknowns, source dates and independent alternatives when evidenced.

## What a candidate includes

- Stable identities for activity, route/segment, facility or overnight option; managing jurisdiction where supported.
- Activity-specific permitted/restricted/unknown evidence, dates/season semantics and claim provenance.
- Route/approach/parking/staging relationships only when source-backed or explicitly reviewed. Distance alone is displayed as straight-line proximity and labeled connection unverified.
- Overnight options distinguished from nearby day-use sites, without inferring camping permission from a listing.
- Current restrictions/closures only when actually checked; “not checked” is explicit.
- Completeness by evidence category, including which questions were not checked; no single misleading verified badge.
- Sources, retrieved-at vs published/effective-at vs reviewed-at, access to original agency information.
- Clear option to open the underlying Explore result and to revisit unresolved questions.

## In scope for version-one product

Colorado trails including hiking, mountain biking, horseback riding and motorized/OHV distinctions; meaningful water (fishing/paddling relevance when supported); land classifications; developed and dispersed-camping context; recreation sites/day-use; agency rules/closures, dates and the identities/relationships to assemble candidates. Usable mobile-first interaction and honest coverage semantics. All claims require approved source rights.

## Not implied by this charter

Transactions or reservations, live availability guarantees, a paid subscription/billing platform, offline-navigation downloads, crowdsourced permission facts, automated legal determinations, a national dataset, a new data backend, real-time vehicle routing guarantees, account/profile system, tracking, autonomous web-agent integrations or local Qwen/Odysseus. These need separate product decisions.

## Owner acceptance scenarios (minimum)

1. **Rampart family outing:** dirt-bike riding plus a nearby plausible family picnic/day-use option; each relationship/limitation explained. A street-legal bike must not silently become a non-street-legal trailer scenario.
2. **Douglas dirt-bike + overnight:** evaluate official riding designations, access constraints, a supported overnight option or a clear unverified state; identify backup when supported.
3. **Aspen hiking + water:** trail, water context, activity-specific unknowns/restrictions and transparent source claims.
4. **Colorado cross-county search:** pan/search seamlessly across at least two previously independent packages; same real-world identity not duplicated based on names alone.
5. **Developed campground + activity:** correct overnight/day-use distinction, published rules, unknown availability and no assumed connector.
6. **Dispersed camping scenario:** no inferred camping permission from public ownership; relevant restrictions visible.
7. **Hostile/stale-data scenario:** a stale restriction remains prominent, zero results do not imply freedom, injected source text cannot override agent or application policy.
8. **Uncovered region:** the map and trip results say what data is absent rather than silently returning “nothing here.”

These are minimum scenario families. Turn each into deterministic fixtures plus independent phone-based user tests. If data coverage cannot establish a viable trip, state the limitation and do not fake one merely to pass.

## Definition of final owner critique

Deliver an accessible staging/product candidate, a scenario-by-scenario outcome report, before/after product scorecard, accurate coverage inventory, supported-source inventory/licensing, known limitations and unclosed trust/security/device gaps. Product critique should be substantive, not a claim that every place in Colorado is legally verified. No release if P0 trust defects or required device gates remain.
