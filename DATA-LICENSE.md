The downloadable JSON is the complete database used by this site. Road way IDs, geographic feature source URLs, and source metadata are retained. Initial data was collected September 19, 2026; additional road extracts were collected September 22, 2026. Roads are a partial local extract. Campground listing notes are short factual paraphrases with links to Recreation.gov; they are not current availability, permission, or road-condition determinations.
